Sticky Notes

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Sticky Notes is a Chrome extension that helps you save notes, tags, categories, favorites, and selected text while browsing.

Information stored

The extension stores the notes and preferences you create, including note text, tags, categories, favorite status, associated website domains, and optional profile image settings.

How information is used

Your information is used only to provide the extension's features: saving notes, organizing them, associating them with websites, restoring backups, and synchronizing your notes through Chrome's storage service.

Data sharing

Sticky Notes does not sell, rent, or share your notes with third parties. The extension does not include advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or an external application account.

Chrome permissions

  • Storage: saves notes and preferences and enables Chrome synchronization.
  • Tabs: identifies the active website so notes can be associated with its domain.
  • Context menus: lets you save text selected on a webpage when you choose the Sticky Notes menu item.

Backups

When you generate a backup, the exported file is downloaded to your device. When you import a backup, the file is read locally by the extension. Backup files are not uploaded to a separate server.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue in the Sticky Notes GitHub repository.
